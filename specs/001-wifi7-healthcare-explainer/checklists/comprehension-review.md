# Comprehension Review Script

**Purpose**: Measure SC-002 (understanding) and SC-010 (depth fits the audience) — quickstart V17.
**Participants**: at least 5 reviewers — at least 2 clinical or executive leaders using **Leadership**
view and at least 2 network or biomedical engineers using **Technical** view.
**Setup**: Each reviewer opens `dist/index.html`, sets the Audience switch, and works through the
6 GHz, MLO, 4K-QAM and TWT tabs at their own pace (about 15 minutes). No coaching during viewing.

## Questions (answer each in one sentence)

1. What does **Multi-Link Operation** let a device do, and why does that help a nurse's badge call?
2. What does the **6 GHz band** add compared with the bands Wi-Fi used before Wi-Fi 6E?
3. What is **Target Wake Time**, and how does it affect battery-powered devices such as badges?
4. What does **4K-QAM** change, and what does it need in order to help?
5. Name **one way Wi-Fi 7 improves on Wi-Fi 6** for a hospital, and the tab where you learned it.

## Depth rating

6. For your role, the level of detail was: ☐ too shallow ☐ about right ☐ too deep

## Scoring

- A question is **correct** when the answer captures the mechanism and one benefit, in any wording.
  Reference answers: (1) one connection uses several bands at once or switches instantly, so calls
  can avoid a congested band; (2) a large, clean block of spectrum with wider channels and no
  older devices; (3) agreed wake-up times so devices can sleep in between, extending battery life;
  (4) more bits per signal for a higher peak rate, which needs a strong, clean signal close to the
  access point; (5) any accurate improvement tied to a healthcare effect.
- **SC-002** passes when at least 80% of reviewers answer each of questions 1–4 correctly.
- **SC-010** passes when at least 80% of all ratings are "about right".
